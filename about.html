About us

The MacEwan University Finance Club (MUFC) is a student-led organization dedicated to providing MacEwan University students with early exposure to finance and related career pathways. Through hands-on and experiential learning opportunities, professional skill development, and mentorship from industry professionals, MUFC aims to bridge the gap between classroom learning and the financial industry. Our vision is to build a strong and inclusive finance community at MacEwan University, where students feel empowered to explore their potential, develop the skills needed to succeed, and confidently pursue careers in the financial industry. Our mission is to empower students with the knowledge, skills, and professional connections needed to succeed academically and pursue meaningful careers in finance. By creating opportunities for education, industry engagement, networking, and community-building, we strive to make finance more accessible while inspiring students to explore and develop their potential within the field.
